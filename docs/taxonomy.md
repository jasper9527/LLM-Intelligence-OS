# Taxonomy

The system uses six tag families:

- `entity_tags`: companies, labs, teams, products, people, venues, repositories.
- `area_tags`: broad technical or market areas.
- `method_tags`: concrete methods, modeling techniques, training approaches, or system patterns.
- `task_tags`: tasks, benchmarks, workflows, and application surfaces.
- `job_tags`: job species and capability clusters seen in hiring markets.
- `signal_tags`: evidence type and interpretation signals.

Required signal tags:

- `signal:negative`
- `signal:breakout`
- `signal:red-flag`
